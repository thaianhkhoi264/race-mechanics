# Skill choice and Stamina planning

## Which skills suit a running style
Skills whose condition contains running_style==N are locked to that style (Front Runner
Straightaways, Pace Chaser Corners, Late Surger Savvy, End Closer Corners, ...). Skills with a
placing condition (order / order_rate) suit the styles whose usual placings overlap it: With All
My Soul (top 3) -> Front Runner and Pace Chaser; Take the Chance and Fighting Spirit (4th or
worse) -> Pace Chaser, Late Surger and End Closer; Eager (5th or worse) -> Late Surger and End
Closer. Skills that only depend on the course (track, direction, season, ground, weather, corners,
straights, slopes), like Right-Handed, Firm Conditions, Professor of Curvature, Swinging Maestro or
Downhill Speedster, help every style on a matching course.

## Skills that are poor for Front Runners
Nimble Navigator and Uma Stan trigger when blocked or boxed in a pack. That suits Pace Chasers,
Late Surgers and End Closers, but a Front Runner who triggers them is already off her plan, so
they are not recommended for Front Runners.

## Groundwork and early skill enablers
Groundwork (acceleration once 3 skills have activated in the early race) helps Front Runners
reach cruising speed sooner and secure the lead. Cheap skills that fire at the very start, such
as Restraint, Ignited Spirit WIT and Prudent Positioning, are taken mainly to reach Groundwork's
3-activation requirement. Tail Held High / Tail Nine work the same way with 3 activations in
mid-race.

## Generally useful skills
Tail Held High, Slipstream and See Ya Later! have no style requirement and give mid-race
velocity for anyone. Racing Spirit: Power (overtaking in the last spurt) and Racing Spirit:
Stamina (last spurt with HP left, costs some HP) are both good; which one depends on whether you
have spare Stamina and enough acceleration to keep overtaking late.

## Minimum effect sizes
A velocity skill is worth it at +0.15 m/s or more (+0.20 for last-spurt velocity). An
acceleration skill should be about +0.2 or more and fire near late-race start.

## Stamina value of recovery and debuffs
A recovery or debuff is a percentage of max HP. Its worth in Stamina points is roughly
pct x (Stamina + distance / (0.8 x style HP coefficient)). On a 2500 m Long course 1% of max HP
is worth about 40 Stamina at 900 Stamina; on a 1600 m Mile it is worth about 25-28. Typical sizes:
standard white recoveries (Straightaway Recovery, Corner Recovery ○, Be Still, Soft Step,
Hydrate, Standing By) 1.5%; Ignited Spirit STA 1.8%; Free-Spirited and All Set 3.5%; gold
recoveries average about 5.3% (Swinging Maestro, Lie in Wait 5.5%); inherited Superior Heal 3.5%.
Average opponent debuffs: gold about 2.6% of max HP, white about 0.96%. A "debuffer" usually
means one opponent landing one gold and one white debuff (about 3.56%).

## Planning Stamina against debuffers
Start from the minimum Stamina for a full spurt on the course and style, then add the Stamina
value of the expected debuffs and subtract the value of recovery skills you can rely on. Recovery
skills only count if their condition is likely to be met in the race.
